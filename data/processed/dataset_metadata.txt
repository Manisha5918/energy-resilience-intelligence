================================================================================
ENERGYSHIELD DGCIS PETROLEUM IMPORT DATASET METADATA
================================================================================

1. DATASET OVERVIEW & DUAL ARCHITECTURE SPECIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EnergyShield maintains two analytically distinct, non-merged DGCIS datasets to
prevent synthetic survivorship bias or false time-series interpolation:

A. HISTORICAL TIME-SERIES DATASET (2019-2026):
   - Source File (Raw): data/raw/DGCIS_Petroleum_Imports_2019-20_to_2025-26_RAW.xlsx
   - Processed Outputs: petroleum_imports_long.csv, petroleum_imports_long.xlsx,
                        petroleum_imports_wide_clean.xlsx
   - Scope: Longitudinal multi-year tracking of India's top 9 strategic sovereign suppliers.
   - Years Covered: 8 Fiscal Years (2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026)
   - Number of Countries: 9 (ANGOLA, CANADA, IRAQ, KUWAIT, NIGERIA, QATAR, RUSSIA,
                             SAUDI ARAB, U S A)
   - Number of HS Codes: 3 (2709, 2710, 2711)
   - Number of Records: 216 records (Long format) / 27 records (Wide format)
   - Total INR Value: INR 6,944,750.2422 Crore
   - Total USD Value: USD 857,646.6828 Million

B. DETAILED 2025-26 ALL-COUNTRIES CENSUS DATASET:
   - Source File (Raw): data/raw/DGCIS_Petroleum_Imports_2025-26_RAW.xlsx
   - Processed Outputs: petroleum_imports_2025_26_all_countries.csv,
                        petroleum_imports_2025_26_all_countries.xlsx
   - Scope: Comprehensive cross-sectional census of all global import origins for FY2025-26.
   - Years Covered: 1 Fiscal Year (2025-26)
   - Number of Countries: 107 partner countries / sovereign territories
   - Number of HS Codes: 3 (2709, 2710, 2711)
   - Number of Records: 172 records
   - Total INR Value: INR 1,504,533.314426 Crore
   - Total USD Value: USD 170,670.076852 Million

2. HS CODES & COMMODITY DEFINITIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   * HS 2709: PETROLEUM OILS AND OILS OBTAINED FROM BITUMINOUS MINERALS, CRUDE
              (Crude oil feedstock for domestic refinery processing)
   * HS 2710: PETROLEUM OILS AND OILS OBTAINED FROM BITUMINOUS MINERALS OTHER THAN CRUDE
              (Refined petroleum products, middle distillates, motor fuels, lube oils)
   * HS 2711: PETROLEUM GASES AND OTHER GASEOUS HYDROCARBONS
              (Liquefied Natural Gas [LNG], Liquefied Petroleum Gas [LPG], ethane/propane)

3. UNITS OF MEASUREMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   - INR_Cr: Indian Rupees in Crores (1 Crore = 10,000,000 INR = 10 Million INR)
   - USD_Mn: United States Dollars in Millions (1 Million USD = 1,000,000 USD)

4. MISSING-VALUE TREATMENT & DATA INTEGRITY POLICY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   - Zero-Fabrication Contract: Genuine missing values in source records are preserved
     strictly as NaN (null/empty), and are NEVER artificially replaced by 0.
   - Zero Distinction: A value of 0.0 indicates a recorded trade value of zero or below
     reporting threshold, whereas NaN indicates no trade return filed for that period.
   - Missing Values in 2025-26 Dataset: 0 missing values (100% complete returns).
   - Missing Values in 2019-2026 Historical Dataset: 7 missing metric pairs (Canada HS 2711
     for 6 years, Nigeria HS 2710 for 2020).

5. SEPARATION RATIONALE & ANALYTICAL INTEGRITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   CRITICAL: The 107-country 2025-26 dataset is intentionally NOT merged into the
   8-year historical time series because historical time-series data for the 98 minor
   partner countries is not present in the historical source table.
   Merging would create artificial sparsity (98 countries x 7 years of NaNs) or falsely
   imply trade existed where unrecorded.
