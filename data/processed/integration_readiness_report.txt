3. RECOMMENDED CANONICAL DATE & FINANCIAL YEAR ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

To maintain rigorous econometric alignment without loss of precision:

1. Canonical Master Calendar:
   - Primary Time Coordinate: Calendar Date `Date` (YYYY-MM-DD)
   - Secondary Mapping Columns:
     * `Month_Year` (YYYY-MM) -> Links directly to Monthly Inflation & FX Monthly Averages
     * `Fiscal_Year_Quarter` (YYYY-YY QX) -> Links directly to Quarterly BoP and Petroleum Trade Account
     * `Financial_Year` (YYYY-YY) -> Links directly to Annual DGCIS Petroleum Imports and Structural FX

2. Temporal Roll-up / Roll-down Protocol:
   - Monthly Inflation & FX rates can be rolled up to Quarterly averages via standard arithmetic mean:
     * Q1: Months 04, 05, 06 (Apr, May, Jun)
     * Q2: Months 07, 08, 09 (Jul, Aug, Sep)
     * Q3: Months 10, 11, 12 (Oct, Nov, Dec)
     * Q4: Months 01, 02, 03 (Jan, Feb, Mar)
   - Quarterly BoP metrics can be aggregated to Financial Year totals (Sum of imports, net trade balance, and weighted CAD % of GDP).

3. Forex Reserves Disciplinary Boundary:
   - The Weekly Forex Reserve series must remain strictly segregated and tagged as `PARTIAL_HISTORICAL_BENCHMARK`.
   - Continuous macroeconomic modeling will utilize BoP Reserves Change / BoP Financial Account rather than uncollected weekly points.
